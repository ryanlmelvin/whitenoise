box-fan.wav
  Original title: box_fan
  Artist: ffdm
  Source: https://freesound.org/people/ffdm/sounds/824852/
  Licensed source preview:
  https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/824/824852_17819872-hq.mp3
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal, as stated on the sound's source page.
  Recording: Lasko box fan on high speed, recorded with an iPhone 14.
  Preparation: Used seconds 36–69 of the recording; mono 24 kHz decode,
  wind-rumble reduction (90 Hz high-pass), 7.5 kHz high-frequency rolloff,
  3-second equal-power endpoint overlap, circular smooth RMS leveling,
  transient gain reduction, smooth peak limiting, normalized to -23 dBFS RMS.
  The exported PCM16 WAV loop is 30 seconds, with no codec padding or silence.

rain.wav
  Original collection: Rain (loopable)
  Original file: 4.ogg from Rain OGG.zip
  Artist: Ylmir
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable
  Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Rain%20OGG.zip
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal, as stated on the source page.
  Preparation: Used seconds 2–35; mono 24 kHz decode, 90 Hz high-pass,
  8.5 kHz high-frequency rolloff, 3-second equal-power endpoint overlap,
  circular smooth RMS leveling, transient gain reduction, smooth peak
  limiting, normalized to -23 dBFS RMS. Exported 30-second PCM16 WAV loop.

white-noise.wav
  Original procedural audio generated for this project; no third-party samples.
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication).
  Deterministic seeded broadband noise, smooth RMS and peak control.
  30-second mono 24 kHz PCM16 WAV, normalized to -23 dBFS RMS.

brown-noise.wav
  Original procedural audio generated for this project; no third-party samples.
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication).
  Deterministic seeded noise with 1/f amplitude shaping (1/f² power), 40 Hz
  low-frequency floor, zero DC, and smooth RMS and peak control.
  30-second mono 24 kHz PCM16 WAV, normalized to -23 dBFS RMS.
